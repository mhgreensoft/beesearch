Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

BeeSearch searches your documents entirely on your device. We don’t collect, upload or sell your documents or personal data.

1. Data BeeSearch accesses

Documents and images in the folders you choose. BeeSearch only reads them to build search data; it never changes or deletes your original files. Edited images, exported PDFs and scans are always saved as new files.

2. Data stored on your device

Index data (text extracted from documents), search history, favorites and your settings. This data stays on your device and is left out of Android’s cloud backup.

To clear the index data of one folder, switch that folder off or remove it in the Status tab. Search history can be cleared on the search screen. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android’s Settings.

3. Text recognition (OCR)

BeeSearch uses Google ML Kit to recognize text. Recognition runs entirely on your device: your images and the text read from them are not sent to Google or to us. Models for Japanese, Chinese, Korean and Devanagari text are downloaded through Google Play services after you turn those scripts on, and ML Kit may contact Google’s servers from time to time for updated models and bug fixes. ML Kit also sends Google metrics about its own performance and use in the app: device model and Android version, the app’s package name and version, an identifier of the device or of the app installation, timings, settings such as image format and resolution, and error codes. Google uses these metrics to measure performance, to debug, maintain and improve ML Kit and to detect misuse, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. According to Google, this data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. The same applies to Google’s document scanner in the Scan tab.

4. Permissions, camera and PIN

Folder access through Android’s folder picker, limited to folders you allow. If App lock is on, your PIN is stored only on your device in hashed form (the PIN itself is never stored).

The Scan tab offers two scanners. BeeSearch’s own scanner needs the camera permission and uses the camera only while its capture screen is open. Google’s scanner is part of Google Play services and uses Google Play services’ camera permission. With both, photos are processed on your device; temporary photos stay in the app’s cache, and the result is saved as a PDF file in the folder you choose.

5. Purchases

BeeSearch is free to use. Bee Search Professional is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under Google’s own terms and privacy policy; we never see your card or other payment details. To know whether you own Bee Search Professional, the app asks Google Play on your phone; the answer, and the number of scans made today for the Bee Search Trial limits, are stored only on your device.

As the seller, we receive Google Play’s order records for each purchase (such as order number, date, price and the buyer’s country) and use them only for accounting, taxes and refunds.

6. Data sharing

BeeSearch doesn’t send your documents, images, searches or index data to anyone. Data leaves the app only in these cases:

  • The technical metrics Google ML Kit sends to Google, described in section 3.
  • When you choose to share a file, open it in another app, or send feedback through your email app.
  • When you search by voice: your speech is handled by the speech recognition service on your phone (usually Google’s) under that service’s own policy; BeeSearch only receives the resulting text.
  • When you buy Bee Search Professional: the purchase goes through Google Play, described in section 5.

7. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top and notify you in the app if the change is significant.
